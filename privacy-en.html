Privacy notice

Updated: 16/11/2026

1. Controller and contact

The controller is Francesco Rodella, Italy. Contact nauteasy@gmail.com for support and privacy rights.

2. Local data and permissions

Tracks, routes, waypoints, boat/engine profiles, settings and offline copies are stored on your device. Location is used for functions that need it, including active recording, navigation and alerts in the background. System permissions can be changed in iOS and are separate from advertising consent. Only attach photos and content that you are entitled to share.

3. Account and shared content

Firebase/Google handles authentication and account email, name or nickname, user identifier and provider. Community, chat, backup and sharing send the required content to Firebase. Published contributions and nicknames are visible to the recipients of the feature. Fleet location sharing is optional and can be stopped with its controls.

4. Weather, maps and providers

The new version uses MET Norway for atmospheric forecasts, truncating coordinates to four decimals. The provider still receives your IP address and the requested coordinates. Local sources (Venice/CPSM, ISPRA, ARPAV and DHMZ where available) and Copernicus Marine have separate coverage. Older releases may still use Open-Meteo; historical copies are not relabelled as the new provider. Maps/search may contact OpenFreeMap, OpenStreetMap/Overpass/OpenSeaMap and Photon. Cloudflare delivers packages and services; MIMIT and AISStream supply their respective features. Search sends query text or map coordinates and your IP address to its provider. Providers receive the technical information required for requests. MapLibre renders maps on the device.

5. Optional AI assistant

In the new version you must explicitly allow sharing before starting an AI analysis. The selected place/coordinates, weather summary and boat/engine profile are sent to Cloudflare Workers AI. Firebase authenticates the NautEasy request. No AI request starts without permission. You may turn it off for subsequent requests; permission resets when you close the AI screen. Do not enter sensitive information. Automated responses can be wrong and do not determine safe navigation.

6. Advertising and subscriptions

Free may show Google AdMob non-personalised banners. Google UMP collects choices where required; reopen them in More → Info and terms → Advertising preferences. No ad requests start before consent checks complete. Non-personalised ads may still process identifiers/technical information for delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. We do not send GPS to banners or intentionally start cross-app tracking. Ads are excluded from navigation, recording, Anchor Watch, alarms and Premium. When available, purchases and renewals are handled by Apple; NautEasy does not receive card numbers. The App Store displays the actual price, period and eligible trial.

7. Purposes, legal bases and transfers

Processing is based on providing requested functions (GDPR Art.6(1)(b)), legal obligations where applicable (c), minimised security/fraud prevention based on legitimate interests (f), and consent for optional AI and advertising processing that requires it (a). Google SDKs may send technical diagnostics, performance and interaction information for their functions even when Firebase Analytics is disabled. Google/Firebase, Cloudflare, Apple and data providers act according to their contractual roles and notices. Processing may occur outside the EEA under applicable provider safeguards; we do not claim that all data stays in Italy. Google privacy · Cloudflare privacy.

8. Retention, export and erasure

Local content remains until deleted; caches are replaced according to data type. Copies exported or saved by recipients cannot be remotely recalled. Export is available through the corresponding app features. An account deletion control exists, but current releases do not automatically erase every cloud category. To complete erasure of backups, chat or associated content contact us: we verify requests and explain any legally required or fraud-prevention retention. We do not promise immediate erasure that has not occurred. Requests are handled within GDPR time limits, with any justified extension communicated.

9. Your rights

You may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability where applicable; withdraw consent for future processing; and complain to the Italian data protection authority (Garante). The service is not aimed at children under14. Candidate features may precede the public release; check your installed version. This notice is updated when service processing changes.

Optional usage analytics

The new version integrates Google Analytics for Firebase, disabled by default. You can grant or withdraw consent in More → Information → Share usage statistics. Refusal does not limit features. With consent we collect section openings, operation outcomes and bucketed timings/sizes; the service uses a pseudonymous installation identifier, technical/session data and may infer an approximate area from the connection. We do not attach GPS coordinates, account IDs, email, chats, photos, search text or boat profiles. Granular location/device collection, Google Signals and Analytics advertising personalization are disabled; advertising consent is separate. These data are not described as anonymous. Identifier-associated event and user data retention is configured to 2 months, without resetting on new activity; standard aggregated reports may be retained differently. Withdrawal stops collection and resets local Analytics data and its identifier; it cannot retract data already received by the provider. Contact us about previously processed data. Test builds do not send events to the production property. Legal basis: consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). Firebase privacy.
