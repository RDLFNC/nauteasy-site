Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Controller

The data controller is Francesco Rodella, an individual based in Italy. Contact: nauteasy@icloud.com.

2. Data we process

NautEasy may process GPS location, navigation data and tracks, waypoints and routes, preferences, technical data, account data (email/name), and content voluntarily submitted to the community. Location is used only when a user activates a feature that requires it, including background location while tracking or navigating.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is used to provide navigation, weather, maps, tracks, authentication, synchronization and community features. The legal basis is service performance; system permissions rely on the user’s consent. Privacy requests can be sent by email.

4. Third parties

Depending on the features used, data and requests may be sent to Firebase/Google, Google Sign-In, Open-Meteo, OpenStreetMap/Overpass, OpenFreeMap, OpenSeaMap, Photon/Nominatim, MIMIT, Cloudflare Workers, AISStream, MapLibre and Google Mobile Ads. Each service has its own terms and privacy notice.

5. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until deleted by the user. Cloud data is retained as needed to provide the service or until deletion is requested. To delete an account and associated data, email nauteasy@icloud.com with the account email; only information needed to verify the request will be requested.

6. Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability where applicable, and may complain to the Italian data protection authority.

7. Children and security

NautEasy is not directed to children under 14. We use HTTPS, authentication and access rules; no online measure is absolute.

8. Changes

This policy may change with the app’s features. The last-update date appears above.

This policy is not legal advice.
