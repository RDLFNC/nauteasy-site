The current line collects the features implemented and verified in the iOS project. BETA and POC items are clearly separated.

Cockpit, GPS and tracking

Position, speed, heading, tracks and route recording in a view designed to remain readable underway.

Navigate and MapLibre

Main MapLibre map with search, waypoints, routes and cartographic layers. MapKit remains a fallback and local search tool.

Weather and sea

Forecasts and marine conditions through Open-Meteo, with transparent caching and source labels.

Tracks, GPX and community

GPX import/export, routes, sharing and community/safety functions in the TestFlight line.

V1 · BETA

AIS, fuel and boat profile

AIS, fuel range, boat profile and maintenance features included in the beta build, with declared limits.

POC

Lagoon channels and routing

Channel rendering and graph based on candidate sources. Not production nautical routing and never a replacement for official sources.
