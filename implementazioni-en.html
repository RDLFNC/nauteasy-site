An Android version is planned after validation of the iOS beta. It is not yet available for download.

Main map with search, waypoints, routes and cartographic layers. MapKit remains a fallback and local search tool.

Weather, sea and tracks

Open-Meteo forecasts, marine conditions, transparent caching, GPX import/export and route recording.

AIS, fuel and boat profile

AIS, fuel range, boat data and maintenance features included in the beta build, with declared limits.

POC

Lagoon channels and routing

Channel rendering and graph based on candidate sources. Not production nautical routing and never a replacement for official sources.
